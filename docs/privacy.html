Deduction

Privacy

Deduction collects nothing, sends nothing and asks for nothing. There is no account, no sign-in and no network connection of any kind.

The short version. The app is a logic puzzle that runs entirely on your phone. It has no servers to talk to, so there is nothing for it to send and nowhere for it to send it.

What is stored

The app keeps a few things on your device so a game survives being put down, and so the statistics screen has something to show:

  • the puzzle you are in the middle of, and the marks you have made on it;
  • which puzzles you have finished, how long each took and how many hints you used;
  • your settings — the colour, the night setting, sound and haptics.

All of it is written to the app's own storage on the device. None of it leaves the phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it, and Settings → Statistics → Clear clears the history without deleting anything else.

What is not collected

No name, no email address, no contacts, no photos, no location, no device identifiers and no usage analytics. The app contains no advertising, no tracking of any kind and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no personal information from anybody, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects or sends anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than quietly made.
