# The cast

A paragraph on each of the seventy people the game can deal you: who they are,
how they hold themselves, and why they carry the hair and the one distinguishing
feature their silhouette is built from.

**None of this is in the app.** What the player reads is the item's label and its
one-line blurb, both of which live in `locales/en-GB.yaml` and are quoted here in
italics. These paragraphs are reference for whoever draws the cast — the two
drawable traits are called out in each of them on purpose, so a redraw has
something to work from beyond a name. See [the construction of the
drawings](../scripts/icons/people.mjs) for how those two traits become a shape.

The **id** is what names the drawing on disk: `assets/icons/<theme>/<category>-<id>.svg`.

## Cosmic Voyage

A pressure collar, sealed square at the throat.

### Rear-Admiral Zarg

`cosmic/astronaut-zarg` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes green · Born on Mars · Height 1.9m

*Has commanded eleven ships and can name none of them.*

Tall, square, and entirely certain. Mars-born, which he mentions. Eleven commands behind him and the ships blur, the crews blur, the medals do not. Wears the crop because a barber is a scheduled appointment and Zarg keeps his appointments, and the beard because nobody outranks him enough to raise it. Stands with his chin up and his weight back, addressing a slightly larger room than the one he is in.

### Cosmonaut Vilenkov

`cosmic/astronaut-vilenkov` · Hair short hair · Distinguishing feature a headset · Eyes grey · Born on Luna · Height 1.7m

*Broadcasts a weather report every morning. There is no weather.*

Neat, unhurried and completely unembarrassed. The headset never comes off — half job, half comfort object — and the boom sits by her mouth at meals. The bulletin goes out at 0600 ship's time to a crew of eleven and a vacuum, read in the level tone of a shipping forecast. Luna-born, so gravity has always been something that happens to other people.

### Space Captain Rodgers

`cosmic/astronaut-rodgers` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes blue · Born on Earth · Height 1.8m

*Insists on a hat with a brim. In a vacuum. For the authority.*

Shaves his head daily and grows the moustache to compensate; it is the whole of his personality above the collar. Insists on a peaked cap with a brim, in a vacuum, on the grounds that authority has a shape. Earth-born and prone to saying so. Carries himself like a portrait of himself — shoulders squared, head level, one hand permanently at the small of his back.

### Dr Halloway

`cosmic/astronaut-halloway` · Hair a ponytail · Distinguishing feature spectacles · Eyes brown · Born on Earth · Height 1.6m

*Prescribes a brisk walk, aboard a vessel forty paces long.*

Ship's physician. Small, brisk, unimpressed, and entirely serious about the walk. Wears the ponytail to keep hair out of a wound and the spectacles because she has read every medical text on board twice. Leans in when she talks to you, and further in when she does not believe you.

### Navigator Okonkwo

`cosmic/astronaut-okonkwo` · Hair braids · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes brown · Born on Ceres · Height 1.7m

*Can find anywhere without the charts, and does, to general alarm.*

Ceres-born, raised on a rock where every direction was down, and left with a sense of position nobody has managed to explain. Wears the braids long and a pair of heavy gold hoops that swing when the ship does. Sits very still while working, then moves in one motion when finished, like somebody who decided ten minutes ago and has been waiting politely.

### Chief Engineer Bolt

`cosmic/astronaut-bolt` · Hair a mohawk · Distinguishing feature an eye patch · Eyes grey · Born on Titan · Height 1.8m

*Fixed the reactor with a spoon. Will not say which spoon.*

Broad, unhurried, and permanently covered in something. Lost the eye to what he describes only as a Tuesday. The mohawk is practical — a helmet seal does not care about the sides — and also a small daily act of defiance against the Chief Engineer's manual. The spoon is in a drawer now, labelled; he will show you the drawer.

### Lieutenant Vex

`cosmic/astronaut-vex` · Hair a topknot · Distinguishing feature mutton chops · Eyes hazel · Born on Europa · Height 1.7m

*Volunteers for everything, especially what nobody volunteered for.*

Europa-born, which explains a certain relish for cold, dark, enclosed places. Wears the topknot high and the mutton chops with real pride; both are, in Vex's view, the mark of a person prepared to go first. Bounces slightly on the balls of the feet while a mission is being described, and has usually volunteered before it is.

### Professor Quill

`cosmic/astronaut-quill` · Hair long hair · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes green · Born on Earth · Height 1.6m

*Studying deep-space silence. Has published nine hundred pages on it.*

Small, soft-voiced and utterly immovable on his subject, which is silence, on which he has published nine hundred pages. The long hair and the long beard have grown at the same rate since roughly 2029, unattended by choice rather than neglect. Sits with his head tilted, listening to something the rest of the crew cannot hear and would not enjoy.

### Ensign Tumble

`cosmic/astronaut-tumble` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature a headset · Eyes blue · Born on Luna · Height 1.5m

*Newest aboard. Has met the same bulkhead on all six decks.*

Newest aboard and smallest aboard, and has now met the same bulkhead on all six decks. Keeps the headset on to be reachable at all times, which is both diligence and a way of never being alone. Luna-born and still adjusting to a floor that stays where it is put. Walks quickly with his head down, which is precisely the problem.

### Commodore Strand

`cosmic/astronaut-strand` · Hair curls · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes grey · Born on Mars · Height 1.9m

*Retired twice. The fleet keeps mislaying the paperwork.*

Tall and heavy-shouldered, with a great grey thatch of curls and a beard never trimmed to regulation because nobody has established which regulation. Has retired twice and been recalled twice, on both occasions by an oversight nobody has volunteered to correct. Sits down slowly, stands up slower, and is right about most things.

### Medic Ondra

`cosmic/astronaut-ondra` · Hair a bun · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes brown · Born on Ganymede · Height 1.7m

*Cheerful in a crisis, which unsettles the crew more than the crisis.*

Ganymede-born, trained in three fields and calm in all of them. The bun is pinned tight for surgery; the moustache is long-standing personal policy. Moves with a steady economy — no wasted step, no raised voice — and keeps up a running commentary through the worst of it that nobody has yet found the moment to stop.

### Cadet Fizz

`cosmic/astronaut-fizz` · Hair an afro · Distinguishing feature a chinstrap · Eyes hazel · Born on Ceres · Height 1.6m

*Sixteen, brilliant, and barred from the helm for enthusiasm.*

The afro is enormous and thoroughly non-regulation, and the chinstrap belongs to a flight helmet worn in corridors, off duty, in case. Ceres-born, so the low gravity of everywhere else is a permanent delight. Never quite stands still, even at attention. Barred from the helm, and taking it extremely well.

### Quartermaster Bex

`cosmic/astronaut-bex` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes brown · Born on Titan · Height 1.8m

*Knows where everything is, and what it costs to find out.*

Titan-born, and runs the stores like a small sovereign nation with its own weather and customs. Shaves her head, and the gold hoops are the only things she owns that are not stock. Arms folded, one eyebrow up, waiting for you to get to the point.

### Flight Sergeant Mo

`cosmic/astronaut-mo` · Hair a bob · Distinguishing feature mutton chops · Eyes blue · Born on Europa · Height 1.9m

*Drills the crew at dawn. There is no dawn. The drill stands.*

Tall and upright and entirely serious about a 0600 line-up in a ship with no windows. Keeps the bob cut square to the jaw and the mutton chops squarer, and inspects both in a mirror before inspecting anybody else. Europa-born. Speaks in short sentences with a full stop you can hear.

## Corner Café

No collar at all — a person in a café is just a person.

### Ms Barley

`cafe/customer-barley` · Hair short hair · Distinguishing feature spectacles · Eyes green · Age 34 · Trade a piano tuner

*Orders the same thing daily and reads the board each time anyway.*

Tunes pianos, which has left her unable to ignore anything slightly out. Neat short hair, plain spectacles, and a canvas roll of felt and steel that goes under the table rather than on it. Sits square to the counter and listens to the grinder rather more than she means to. Reads the whole board every day, in case.

### Alderman Crumb

`cafe/customer-crumb` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes grey · Age 61 · Trade a retired alderman

*Has opinions about the paving and will share them, unprompted.*

Retired from the council and not at all from the opinions. Has views on the paving, the bins, the one-way system and the new awning, and will share them with anybody within range. Shaved head, magnificent grey moustache, and a lapel that still expects a badge. Leans back with both hands flat on the table, like a man about to open a meeting.

### Mrs Marzipan

`cafe/customer-marzipan` · Hair a bob · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes brown · Age 58 · Trade a piano teacher

*Brings her own cup. Staff have stopped arguing about it.*

Teaches piano to eleven children, of whom two practise. Neat bob, pearl hoops, and a pencil somewhere in the hair. Sits very upright with her hands in her lap, tapping a rhythm on the table without knowing she is doing it. The cup is her own — proper china, saucer and all — and that argument was settled years ago.

### Dr Frangipane

`cafe/customer-frangi` · Hair a ponytail · Distinguishing feature spectacles · Eyes hazel · Age 43 · Trade a dentist

*Marks essays at the corner table and sighs at every one.*

A dentist who marks his evening-class essays at the corner table. Wears the ponytail because a dentist's hair has nowhere else to be, and the spectacles because he has spent twenty years eight inches from his work. Sits with the pen already uncapped and a red mark ready. Has never once finished the coffee while it is hot.

### PC Bap

`cafe/customer-bap` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature a headset · Eyes blue · Age 29 · Trade a police constable

*On the beat, off the clock, and never quite either.*

Young, square-cropped, and sitting facing the window with his back straight. The earpiece stays in and the shoulder set stays on even during a break, which is why the break never quite happens. Stands up whenever somebody outside walks past a little too quickly, then sits down again, slightly embarrassed.

### Reverend Crumpet

`cafe/customer-crumpet` · Hair long hair · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes brown · Age 52 · Trade a vicar

*Writes Sunday sermons here. They get funnier by the cup.*

Long grey-brown hair and a beard the parish council has twice raised as an item. Collar on, cardigan over it. Writes Sunday's sermon here in a small hand, crosses out a great deal, and laughs at his own drafts loudly enough that the counter has stopped looking up. They do get funnier by the cup.

### Sergeant Bloomer

`cafe/customer-bloomer` · Hair a mohawk · Distinguishing feature an eye patch · Eyes grey · Age 47 · Trade an army sergeant

*Sits facing the door. Says it is habit; it is not.*

A short greying mohawk that predates the army and survived it, and a patch worn without explanation or self-consciousness. Takes the chair by the door before the coat is off. Orders quickly, eats quickly, and reads the room on the way in. Perfectly pleasant company once seated.

### Miss Custard

`cafe/customer-custard` · Hair braids · Distinguishing feature a chinstrap · Eyes green · Age 9 · Trade a schoolgirl

*Nine years old, allowed one hot chocolate, negotiates for two.*

Two tight braids and a cycle helmet that does not come off indoors, chinstrap and all, because taking it off is a step towards going home. Kneels on the chair rather than sitting on it. Has a written case prepared, in pencil, for the second hot chocolate, and has been practising it on the walk over.

### Prof Sourdough

`cafe/customer-sourdough` · Hair curls · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes blue · Age 70 · Trade a professor of logic

*Came in for ten minutes in 2011 and has been thinking since.*

A professor of logic, retired in name only, with a white cloud of curls and a beard that has merged with it. One book, face down, unread for an hour at a time. Sits absolutely still, writes a single line, then sits absolutely still again. The ten minutes were in 2011 and the thinking is ongoing.

### Mr Filo

`cafe/customer-filo` · Hair a topknot · Distinguishing feature mutton chops · Eyes brown · Age 38 · Trade a locksmith

*Reads the paper back to front and the crossword not at all.*

A locksmith — a trade of small patient movements, and it shows in everything he does. Works through the paper from the sport forwards, and never once the crossword. Wears the topknot high and the mutton chops with the unbothered confidence of a man never once asked about them. Turns the cup a quarter-turn before each sip.

### Nurse Nutmeg

`cafe/customer-nutmeg` · Hair a bun · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes hazel · Age 26 · Trade a night nurse

*Off a night shift, drinking something the size of a bucket.*

Hair in the same bun it has been in for fourteen hours, hoops still in, tunic still on under the coat. Sits low in the chair with both hands round something the size of a bucket, and the thousand-yard cheerfulness of somebody who will be asleep by eleven in the morning.

### Councillor Praline

`cafe/customer-praline` · Hair an afro · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes brown · Age 55 · Trade a councillor

*Holds a surgery by the window. Nobody has ever booked one.*

Holds a weekly constituency surgery at the window table, with a folder, a pen and an entirely undimmed sense of civic purpose, and a diary four years empty. A fine afro and a finer moustache, both maintained. Sits facing outward so as to be available, and greets people who are simply walking past.

### Mrs Battenberg

`cafe/customer-battenberg` · Hair a bun · Distinguishing feature a chinstrap · Eyes grey · Age 81 · Trade a champion knitter

*Knits through anything, including a small kitchen fire.*

Eighty-one, a champion knitter three counties over, and knits through absolutely anything — including, once, a small kitchen fire. Silver bun, and a scarf tied under the chin against a draught only she can feel. The needles do not stop while she talks, and she talks a good deal. Watches the door over the top of the work.

### Captain Tuille

`cafe/customer-tuille` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature an eye patch · Eyes blue · Age 64 · Trade a merchant captain

*Retired from the merchant navy. Still calls the counter the galley.*

Shaved head, black patch, forearms like mooring rope. Forty years in the merchant navy have left him calling the counter the galley, the toilets the heads and the manager the mate. Sits with his back to the wall out of habit. Perfectly cheerful, extremely loud, and known by name to people he has not met.

## The Long Quest

A pauldron over the near shoulder and a clasp at the throat.

### Sir Brannoch

`quest/hero-brannoch` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes blue · Calling a knight · Age 44

*Sworn to slay one beast. Has slain it. Will not stop mentioning it.*

A big square knight going comfortably to seed, with a shaved scalp under the helm and a beard kept since the day in question. Stands with one foot forward as though a portrait were imminent. Genuinely brave and genuinely exhausting, in roughly equal measure. The beast does come up.

### Dame Sorrel

`quest/hero-sorrel` · Hair a ponytail · Distinguishing feature an eye patch · Eyes green · Calling an archer · Age 31

*Never misses. Has been asked not to demonstrate indoors again.*

Lean, still, and visibly bored by any conversation that is not about distance. Wears the hair in a high ponytail so a bowstring has nowhere to catch, and the patch over the eye she does not aim with — which she maintains is an advantage and will show you, given a corridor and a moment unsupervised.

### Squire Ivo

`quest/hero-ivo` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature a chinstrap · Eyes brown · Calling a squire · Age 17

*Carries everything. Has never once been asked what he thinks.*

Seventeen, and carries everything. Hair cropped short under a helm a size too large, chinstrap buckled tight to stop it turning. Walks half a pace behind and slightly loaded to one side. Watches everything, says almost nothing, and misses nothing at all.

### Hedge-Witch Wren

`quest/hero-wren` · Hair curls · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes hazel · Calling a witch · Age 68

*Cures warts, curdles milk, and is vague about which is which.*

Sixty-eight, wild grey curls, and a moustache she regards as nobody's business but her own. Lives at the edge of the wood and the edge of the law. Sits down by other people's fires without being invited and is never quite asked to leave. The warts do clear up, eventually, mostly.

### Warden Tamsin

`quest/hero-tamsin` · Hair braids · Distinguishing feature mutton chops · Eyes grey · Calling a ranger · Age 39

*Knows every path in the wood and admits to roughly half.*

Grey-streaked braids pulled back, heavy side-whiskers, and a coat the colour of everything. Speaks rarely, moves quietly, and has an unnerving habit of already being where you were going. Answers questions three or four seconds later than anybody expects, and only about half of them.

### Archmage Kell

`quest/hero-kell` · Hair long hair · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes grey · Calling a mage · Age 77

*Can turn lead to gold. Has turned a great deal of gold to lead.*

Seventy-seven, with white hair and a white beard of equal and considerable length, both moving independently of the weather. Stands leaning on a staff he does not need. Delivers bad news with obvious relish and good news as though it were a technicality. The matter of the gold is not discussed.

### Brother Rowan

`quest/hero-rowan` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature spectacles · Eyes brown · Calling a monk · Age 54

*Sworn to silence. Communicates entirely through pointed sighing.*

Tonsured to a shave, small round reading spectacles on a cord, hands permanently ink-stained from the scriptorium. Sits with a straight back and folded hands. Sworn to silence, and manages without a single word to convey precise and highly detailed disappointment, mostly through the shoulders.

### Bard Fenn

`quest/hero-fenn` · Hair a topknot · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes green · Calling a bard · Age 26

*Writes the ballad before the quest, then bends the quest to fit.*

Twenty-six, topknot, a hoop in each ear, and an instrument that is always slightly in the way. Talks with both hands. Has already decided which of the party is the hero of the piece, and it is not the one anybody would expect — and the ballad, being written, is now somewhat load-bearing.

### Baroness Maeve

`quest/hero-maeve` · Hair a bun · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes blue · Calling a noble · Age 49

*Funds every expedition and turns up uninvited to all of them.*

Hair pinned into a hard bun, hoops the size of bracelets, and riding clothes rather than court ones. Arrives with three servants and no warning whatever. Funds every expedition in the province and turns up uninvited to all of them. Enormously good company, and completely impossible to refuse.

### Reeve Osric

`quest/hero-osric` · Hair short hair · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes brown · Calling a reeve · Age 58

*Counts the takings twice. The second count is the one he reports.*

Neat, plain and forgettable — short hair, trimmed moustache, an unremarkable coat — and all of it entirely deliberate. Stands with his hands clasped in front of him. Has been the most powerful person in the village for eleven years and nobody has noticed, which is the arrangement he prefers.

### Apprentice Perrin

`quest/hero-perrin` · Hair a mohawk · Distinguishing feature a headset · Eyes hazel · Calling a mage · Age 19

*Learning spellcraft by trial. Mostly trial.*

Nineteen, with a mohawk that was singed into existence rather than chosen, and a listening-horn strapped over one ear for lessons conducted at a safe distance. Enthusiastic, scorched at the cuffs, and genuinely improving. Leans forward at all times, including while sitting down.

### Herald Isolde

`quest/hero-isolde` · Hair a bob · Distinguishing feature a chinstrap · Eyes blue · Calling a herald · Age 35

*Announces arrivals. Has announced two that had not arrived.*

A precise blunt bob, a strap under the chin holding a tabard's hood, and a voice trained to carry the length of a courtyard. Stands very straight with her chin slightly up. Recovering, with dignity, from an incident involving a delegation, a fog, and two arrivals that had not arrived.

### Sellsword Garrick

`quest/hero-garrick` · Hair an afro · Distinguishing feature mutton chops · Eyes brown · Calling a sellsword · Age 41

*Loyal for the length of the contract, and courteous after it.*

A big afro, heavy side-whiskers, and a professional's calm about the whole business. Reads the terms carefully and keeps them exactly. Sits where he can see the door, orders the cheapest thing on offer, and is the last person in the room worth worrying about — until the term ends.

### Captain Nyla

`quest/hero-nyla` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature an eye patch · Eyes green · Calling a soldier · Age 33

*Leads from the front, which is how she keeps losing the map.*

Cropped hair, a patch, and a habit of setting off before the sentence explaining where has finished. Her soldiers adore her; her quartermaster does not. Stands with her weight on the front foot and one hand already on the door. The map is, currently, somewhere behind her.

## Reef Dive

A wetsuit collar, rolled high.

### Skipper Shoal

`reef/diver-shoal` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes grey · Known for wreck work · Height 1.8m

*Has named every boat after the last one that sank.*

Shaved scalp, salt-white beard, and the permanent squint of forty years of glare off water. Works wrecks, and knows of several that are on no chart. Stands at the wheel with his feet apart and never holds on to anything. The naming of the boats is either sentiment or accounting and he will not say which.

### Bosun Pike

`reef/diver-pike` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature mutton chops · Eyes brown · Known for rope work · Height 1.7m

*Ties knots in his sleep. The crew check their bootlaces each morning.*

Close crop, heavy side-whiskers, hands like tackle blocks. Rope is his entire subject and he will explain it whether or not a question has been asked. Sits on a gunwale rather than a seat, always with something in his hands that is being worked, and hands it back finished.

### Dr Ferrand

`reef/diver-ferrand` · Hair a ponytail · Distinguishing feature spectacles · Eyes green · Known for marine science · Height 1.6m

*Counting urchins for science. Up to four hundred thousand.*

Small, dry and entirely serious, with the hair tied back and the spectacles pushed up on her head for at least half of every conversation. Carries a slate and pencil at all times — including on land, including at dinner. The count stands at four hundred thousand and she is unwilling to round.

### Divemaster Tobin

`reef/diver-tobin` · Hair short hair · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes blue · Known for teaching · Height 1.8m

*Briefs for forty minutes on a dive that lasts twenty.*

Short hair, a moustache of great personal significance, and a laminated card for every conceivable eventuality. Draws diagrams in the air with both hands and does not notice people sitting down. Thorough to a fault, and the fault is the briefing, which outlasts the dive by twenty minutes.

### Ranger Adaeze

`reef/diver-adaeze` · Hair braids · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes brown · Known for patrolling · Height 1.7m

*Guards the reef. Has memorised every fine in the by-laws.*

Long braids and heavy hoops, and a whistle she has not yet had to use. Polite, immovable, and entirely willing to recite subsection four from memory, including the amounts. Walks the boat slowly from bow to stern while she talks to you, and you follow. Nobody has yet argued with her twice.

### Cadet Nico

`reef/diver-nico` · Hair a mohawk · Distinguishing feature a headset · Eyes hazel · Known for nothing yet · Height 1.5m

*Newly qualified. Checks the tank gauge roughly every nine seconds.*

Small and wiry, with a mohawk that survives a day under a hood and a comms headset kept on for the reassurance of a voice. Known for nothing yet, and painfully aware of it. Nods a great deal more than is strictly necessary.

### First Mate Marlow

`reef/diver-marlow` · Hair a shaved head · Distinguishing feature an eye patch · Eyes grey · Known for deep dives · Height 1.9m

*Second in command and first into the water, every time.*

Very tall, shaved head, black patch, and a deliberate slowness on deck that becomes something else entirely below thirty metres. Says little. When he does speak the boat goes quiet, which he finds mildly embarrassing and has stopped trying to prevent. Second in the roster, first over the side.

### Chief Indra

`reef/diver-indra` · Hair a bun · Distinguishing feature a chinstrap · Eyes brown · Known for logistics · Height 1.6m

*Runs the compressor, the roster and, quietly, the whole island.*

A tight practical bun and a strap under the chin holding a sunhat that has never once blown off. Carries a clipboard as a matter of principle rather than necessity — it is all memorised anyway. Everybody asks her first, about everything, including a good deal that is not hers.

### Prof Cleo

`reef/diver-cleo` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature spectacles · Eyes green · Known for octopus work · Height 1.7m

*Studies octopus behaviour. Reports being studied back.*

Practical crop, heavy-framed spectacles, and a way of describing an animal's opinions that colleagues long ago stopped correcting. Sits on the deck with her back against the gunwale and the notebook on her knees, entirely absorbed. Maintains, in print, that the octopus is taking notes as well.

### Instructor Bo

`reef/diver-bo` · Hair an afro · Distinguishing feature a moustache · Eyes blue · Known for teaching · Height 1.8m

*Teaches beginners with enormous patience and no volume control.*

A big afro that fills a hood, a fine moustache, and a laugh audible from the mooring. Nobody has ever been frightened in his class, which is exactly the point. Demonstrates everything twice, then once more for the person at the back, at a volume the whole bay can follow.

### Ms Yara

`reef/diver-yara` · Hair long hair · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes brown · Known for photography · Height 1.6m

*Photographs everything. Has nine thousand pictures of the same eel.*

Long hair, and hoops that catch on a hood about once a dive. Will wait ninety minutes for a fish to turn around and does not consider this waiting. Reviews the day's frames at dinner and shows them to people who have already seen them. Nine thousand of the nine thousand are the same eel.

### Reverend Elias

`reef/diver-elias` · Hair curls · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes grey · Known for night dives · Height 1.9m

*Blesses each boat before it leaves. The boats keep coming back.*

Very tall, grey curls, full beard, and a fondness for night diving his congregation has never quite been able to place. Speaks slowly, and looks at the water while he does it. The boats do keep coming back, which he does not offer as evidence but has never troubled to discourage.

### Medic Suki

`reef/diver-suki` · Hair a topknot · Distinguishing feature a chinstrap · Eyes hazel · Known for first aid · Height 1.5m

*Carries a kit for every disaster. Has used the plasters.*

Small and fast, hair up in a topknot, chinstrap from a helmet worn on deck ever since one incident with a boom. Optimistic about people and deeply pessimistic about equipment. Repacks the kit every single evening. In four seasons the plasters are the only thing she has needed.

### Old Rafa

`reef/diver-rafa` · Hair a bob · Distinguishing feature mutton chops · Eyes blue · Known for record depths · Height 1.7m

*Dived here before the reef had a name. Disputes the name.*

A grey bob cut with kitchen scissors, side-whiskers of considerable age, and a set of depth records nobody has verified or dared to question. Sits at the stern with a cigarette he never lights. Corrects the young ones rarely, and devastatingly. Was here first, and disputes the name.

## Village Show

An open collar with the lapel turned back.

### Ms Rose

`garden/gardener-rose` · Hair a bun · Distinguishing feature hoop earrings · Eyes green · Age 62 · Best prize Best in Show

*Grows the finest blooms in the village and will not say how.*

Sixty-two, silver bun, small gold hoops, secateurs in a hip pocket at all hours. Takes Best in Show with a regularity described at the parish council as a matter for the committee. Answers questions about method with questions about the weather, and has never once been caught out.

### Vicar Parsley

`garden/gardener-parsley` · Hair short hair · Distinguishing feature a beard · Eyes brown · Age 55 · Best prize a Highly Commended

*Blesses the marrows. The marrows have not disappointed him yet.*

A tidy short back and sides, a full brown beard, and a dog collar worn under a gardening jumper with a hole in the elbow. Highly Commended four years running and entirely at peace with it. Kneels to weed and stays down a while. Blesses the marrows every August, without irony.

### Master Mint

`garden/gardener-mint` · Hair a crop · Distinguishing feature a headset · Eyes hazel · Age 11 · Best prize nothing at all

*Eleven years old and already undercutting everyone at the stall.*

Cropped hair and headphones on at all times, including while making change. Sits on an upturned crate with a cash tin and a hand-lettered sign, undercutting the entire row. Has never won anything, having never entered — the prize money being, in his assessment, worse than the trade.

### Mrs Petunia
